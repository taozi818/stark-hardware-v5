# V5 Multilingual AI Localization Engine

## Supported Languages
- English (EN)
- Arabic (AR)
- Spanish (ES)

## Generation Rules

### English
Target: Global B2B buyers
- Professional industrial tone
- SEO optimized headings
- Alibaba.com style product language

### Arabic
Target: Middle East markets
- RTL layout support
- Localized business expressions
- GCC buyer preference optimization
- WhatsApp inquiry CTA support

### Spanish
Target: Latin America and Spain
- Localized commercial Spanish
- Regional keyword adaptation
- B2B purchasing intent optimization

## Output Structure

Generate:
- /en/
- /ar/
- /es/

Each language package includes:
- Homepage
- Product pages
- About company
- FAQ
- Contact
- SEO metadata
- Schema markup

## Translation Quality Rules

Do not perform literal translation.
Adapt:
- Buyer psychology
- Search keywords
- Industry terminology
- Cultural expectations
