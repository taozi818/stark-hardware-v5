# SEO/GEO Checklist

Required:
- unique title
- meta description
- canonical
- semantic headings
- sitemap.xml
- robots.txt
- llms.txt

Structured data:
- Organization
- Product
- FAQPage
- BreadcrumbList

Content:
- factual product specifications
- clear company identity
- contact information
- application/use cases
- FAQ
- internal links

Do not claim guaranteed rankings, indexing, or AI citation.
